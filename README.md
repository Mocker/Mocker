# Ryan Guthrie (Mocker)
**Staff Software Architect & Engineering Lead**

With over 20 years of engineering experience across cloud infrastructure, high-throughput backend scaling, and modern web architecture, I specialize in turning complex organizational requirements into clean, low-maintenance software that just works. 

I approach system design through **first-principles engineering**—evaluating technical solutions via logic and scientific evaluation rather than industry hype or dogma.

---

### 🧠 Architectural Philosophy

* **First-Principles & Low-Energy Maintenance:** Designing architectures with minimal moving parts, favoring zero-bloat static site generation, clean system boundaries, and zero-dependency utilities over unnecessary complexity.
* **Cross-Functional Leadership:** Guiding engineering initiatives from ambiguity to execution—aligning product strategy, analytics, and software architecture around reliable delivery.
* **Data Independence & Decentralization:** Decoupling data from proprietary vendor ecosystems, advocating for open protocols (like Nostr), and eliminating vendor lock-in.
* **Agentic AI Orchestration:** Seamlessly integrating agentic coding workflows and LLM tooling directly into daily development and deployment pipelines.
